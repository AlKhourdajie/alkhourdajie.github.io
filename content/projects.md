^Horizon Europe, concludes November 2026
Work Package 4 lead. The work package develops and runs scenarios in which mitigation pathways meet compound disruptions, including extreme heat and interruptions to trade.

^Horizon Europe, completed
Work Package 4 lead. The project expanded integrated assessment modelling through co-production with stakeholders and multi-model comparison in support of the Global Stocktake.

:::section 2
## Scientific assessments

:::rows
**[IPCC Seventh Assessment Report, Working Group III](https://www.ipcc.ch/assessment-report/ar7/)**
Lead Author, Chapter 14 on integration and interactions across sectors and systems, 2025 to 2029. Leads the chapter section on digitalisation, leads the Working Group III cross-chapter assessment of carbon capture and storage and carbon capture and utilisation, and leads a cross-Working Group assessment of geological carbon dioxide storage. Contributes to cross-Working Group assessments on the ethical governance of solar radiation modification, on net zero, on overshoot, and on tipping elements.

**[World Climate Research Programme](https://www.wcrp-climate.org/slc-activities/tpa)**
Lead Author, assessment of high-impact climate events, tipping points and irreversible regional impacts, 2024 to 2027. Co-leads Section 7 on threshold breaching and tipping points in socioecological impacts.

**[IPCC Task Group on Data](https://www.ipcc.ch/data/)**
Member for the Seventh Assessment cycle, 2025 to present. Ex-officio member through the Sixth Assessment cycle, 2019 to 2024.

**[UN Environment Programme, Global Environment Outlook 7](https://www.unep.org/geo/)**
Lead Author, Chapters 9, 10, 11 and 19, 2022 to 2025.

**[IPCC Sixth Assessment Report, Working Group III](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009157926)**
Editor of the report, Coordinating Lead Author of Annex II, Contributing Author to Chapters 2, 3 and 12, 2019 to 2023. Senior Scientist in the Technical Support Unit, 2019 to 2022.
:::
